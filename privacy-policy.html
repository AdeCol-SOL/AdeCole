Privacy Policy

At AdeCole Digital, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our website.

1. Information We Collect

AdeCole Digital may collect personal information when visitors voluntarily submit a consultation request.

This information may include your name, email address, and details about your business or enquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information submitted through our website to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective clients, and provide information about our services.

Consultation requests submitted through our website are processed using Formspree, a third-party form submission service. Information provided through the consultation form is transmitted to Formspree for processing and delivery to AdeCole Digital.

3. Website Analytics and Consent

AdeCole Digital uses Google Analytics 4 to understand website interactions and improve visitor experience.

Analytics cookies are optional. Analytics tracking remains disabled unless a visitor explicitly accepts optional analytics cookies. Visitors can accept or reject analytics through our consent banner. Their preferences can be changed using the Privacy Settings button.

4. Third-Party Services

AdeCole Digital uses third-party services to support website functionality and communication.

These services include Google Analytics 4 for website analytics, Google Tag Manager for managing tracking technologies, and Formspree for processing consultation enquiries.

These third-party providers may process information on behalf of AdeCole Digital to deliver thier respective services. Thier handling of personal information is also governed by thier applicable privacy policies and contractual data prottection obligations.

5. Data Retention

AdeCole Digital retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Consultation enquiries that do not result in a business relationship will be retained for a maximum of 12 months after the last communication concerning the enquiry.

Where no further communication takes place, the 12-month retention period begins on the date the enquiry was submitted.

After this period, personal information associated with the enquiry will be deleted or anonymised, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

Information relating to ongoing business relationships may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary to provide services or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, visitors may have the right to request access to their personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of their information.

Visitors may also withdraw their consent to optional website analytics at any time using the Privacy Settings button.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact AdeCole Digital at adesolacole73@gmail.com .
