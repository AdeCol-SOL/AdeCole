DATA ANALYTICS CASE STUDY

Bellabeat Smart Device Behavior Analysis

Exploring activity, sleep and sedentary behavior to identify patterns that can inform data-driven wellness engagement and marketing strategy.

Python · Pandas · Tableau

BUSINESS QUESTION

How can smart-device behavior inform Bellabeat's customer engagement strategy?

The analysis examines how users behave across activity, sleep and sedentary measures to identify patterns that may reveal opportunities for more relevant wellness engagement.

DATA SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Understanding the boundaries of the analysis.

The analysis uses publicly available smart-device activity and sleep data. Activity records represent 35 participants, while sleep records represent 24 participants, with 410 matched activity-sleep records used for the combined analysis.

Because the dataset represents a relatively small sample and does not provide sufficient demographic information to establish whether participants are representative of Bellabeat's target customers, the findings should be interpreted as behavioral signals rather than population-wide conclusions.

The findings therefore identify patterns and potential engagement opportunities that should be validated using larger, more representative first-party datasets.

KEY FINDINGS

What the behavioral data revealed.

Activity varies
across the week

Average daily steps peaked on Tuesday at 8,668 steps, with Saturday close behind at 8,667. Sunday recorded the lowest average at 7,457 steps — approximately 16% below Tuesday.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Lower-activity periods may provide an opportunity to test timely motivational messaging or activity prompts.

♦

Higher-intensity activity
aligns with calorie expenditure

Very active minutes showed a positive correlation with daily calories burned (r = 0.61), indicating that higher-intensity activity tended to coincide with greater calorie expenditure.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Bellabeat could test personalized activity goals and progress messaging that connects active behavior with measurable wellness outcomes.

Sleep duration
and efficiency vary

Average sleep duration was highest on Sunday at approximately 7h 33m, compared with 6h 41m on Thursday — a difference of about 51 minutes. Sunday also recorded the lowest average sleep efficiency (90.5%), while Thursday recorded the highest (92.3%).

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Sleep duration and sleep efficiency should be treated as separate behaviors in engagement and messaging.

Sedentary time is associated with shorter sleep

Sedentary minutes showed a moderate negative correlation with total minutes asleep (r = -0.60) across the matched activity-sleep records. Higher sedentary time therefore tended to coincide with shorter sleep duration.

CORRELATION COEFFICIENT r = -0.60

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Bellabeat could test interventions that encourage users to break up prolonged sedentary periods while measuring the impact on sleep.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Turn behavioral signals into relevant engagement.

The analysis identifies behavioral patterns that Bellabeat could use to design and test more relevant wellness engagement. Interventions could respond to activity, sleep and sedentary behavior while measuring whether they improve engagement and wellness outcomes.

01

Activate lower-activity periods

Test timely motivational prompts during historically lower-activity periods, particularly around Sunday behavior.

Measure: steps after prompts and engagement rate.

02

Personalize activity goals

Use activity intensity and recent behavior to test personalized goals and progress messages.

Measure: active minutes, goal completion and app engagement.

03

Support sleep consistency

Test sleep reminders informed by recent sleep patterns and day-of-week variation.

Measure: sleep duration, consistency and reminder engagement.

04

Reduce prolonged sedentary periods

Test sedentary-break reminders and evaluate whether changes in sedentary behavior are accompanied by changes in sleep.

Measure: sedentary minutes, sleep duration and response rate.

MEASUREMENT & NEXT STEPS

Turn behavioral insights into testable growth opportunities.

01

Segment

Group users by behavioral signals such as activity level, sedentary time, sleep patterns and device engagement to support more relevant messaging.

02

Personalize

Test personalized wellness messages such as activity encouragement, sedentary-time reminders and sleep-support content.

03

Measure

Track engagement with interventions using metrics such as notification interaction, app engagement, feature adoption and subsequent behavioral change.

04

Experiment

Use controlled experiments to determine whether personalized interventions improve engagement and wellness behaviors compared with standard messaging.
