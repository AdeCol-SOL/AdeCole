DIGITAL MARKETING ANALYTICS CASE STUDY

Marketing Channel Performance Analysis

Analyzing digital marketing channel performance across 901,097 website sessions and 12,070 transactions to evaluate conversion effectiveness, revenue contribution, and marketing efficiency. This Excel-based analysis identifies high-performing acquisition channels and opportunities to improve marketing outcomes through data-driven decision-making.

Microsoft Excel · PivotTables · Data Visualization · Marketing Analytics

BUSINESS QUESTION

Which marketing channels generate the greatest revenue relative to their traffic volume, and where are the strongest opportunities to improve conversion performance?

This analysis evaluates website traffic, transactions, and revenue across eight digital marketing channels. Using Microsoft Excel, the project examines conversion rates, average order values, and revenue efficiency to identify high-performing channels and opportunities for marketing optimization.

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From raw behavioral data to actionable insight.

01

Prepare

Organized and summarized marketing performance data across eight digital marketing channels using Microsoft Excel and PivotTables.

02

Analyze

Calculated conversion rates, average order values, revenue per session, traffic share, revenue share, and the Revenue Efficiency Index to compare performance across eight marketing channels.

03

Visualize

Developed an Excel performance dashboard featuring four KPI summaries and four visualizations to compare marketing channel revenue efficiency, traffic share, conversion rates, and average order values. Translated the findings into actionable business recommendations.

04

Recommend

Developed actionable marketing recommendations based on channel conversion performance, revenue contribution, and revenue efficiency. Identified opportunities to strengthen referral partnerships, optimize organic and social traffic, and investigate scaling high-performing Display campaigns.

DATA SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Understanding the boundaries of the analysis.

This analysis examines marketing channel performance across eight acquisition channels, using sessions, transactions, and revenue to evaluate traffic contribution, conversion efficiency, average order value, and revenue generation. The dataset contains 901,097 sessions, 12,070 transactions, and total revenue of 1,531,766.30.

The dataset does not include advertising expenditure, customer acquisition costs, campaign-level targeting information, or sufficient attribution data to determine the complete contribution of each marketing channel. Consequently, revenue efficiency should not be interpreted as return on advertising spend (ROAS) or profitability.

The findings identify differences in channel performance and opportunities for further investigation. Recommendations should be validated using campaign-level performance data, acquisition costs, attribution insights, and controlled experiments before major marketing investment decisions are made.

KEY FINDINGS

What the marketing performance data revealed.

Display Advertising delivers
the highest revenue efficiency

Display Advertising generates 5.11% of total revenue from just 0.69% of website sessions. Its Revenue Efficiency Index of 7.40 is the highest across all eight marketing channels, while its Average Order Value of $518.56 also leads the channel comparison.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Display Advertising generates substantial revenue relative to its traffic volume. Further investigation should identify the campaigns, audiences, and placements responsible for this performance. Scaling decisions should be supported by advertising cost and profitability data.

♦

Referral drives the highest
conversion rate and revenue share

Referral accounts for 11.59% of website sessions but generates 42.20% of total revenue. It achieves the highest conversion rate at 5.28%, producing 5,514 transactions. Its Revenue Efficiency Index of 3.64 demonstrates strong revenue generation relative to traffic volume.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Referral is a major revenue contributor and attracts visitors who convert at a relatively high rate. Identifying the referral sources responsible for this performance could reveal opportunities to strengthen existing partnerships and develop new relationships with similarly high-performing referral sources.

Organic Search generates high traffic
but lower revenue efficiency

Organic Search contributes 42.19% of total website sessions, making it the largest traffic acquisition channel. However, it generates only 21.26% of total revenue, with a conversion rate of 0.94% and a Revenue Efficiency Index of 0.50.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Organic Search attracts substantial website traffic, but its relatively low conversion rate suggests opportunities to improve conversion performance. Analyzing search intent, landing-page relevance, user experience, and conversion pathways could help identify barriers to completing transactions. Controlled A/B testing can be used to evaluate potential improvements.

Social attracts substantial traffic
but generates limited revenue

Social contributes 25.07% of total website sessions, representing 225,904 visits. However, it generates only 0.32% of total revenue and records 131 transactions. Its conversion rate is approximately 0.06%, with a Revenue Efficiency Index of 0.01.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Social attracts significant website traffic but contributes relatively little directly attributed revenue. Further analysis should investigate audience quality, campaign objectives, landing-page relevance, and conversion pathways. Assisted conversions and attribution should also be evaluated before making budget allocation decisions.

STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning marketing performance insights into actionable growth strategies.

This analysis identifies opportunities to improve marketing performance across eight digital acquisition channels. The recommendations prioritize strengthening high-performing channels, improving conversion efficiency, and investigating underperforming traffic sources. Each recommendation is informed by channel-level revenue, conversion rates, traffic contribution, and the Revenue Efficiency Index. Further evaluation of advertising costs and attribution is necessary before making budget allocation decisions.

01

Investigate and Scale High-Efficiency Display Advertising

Display Advertising generates 5.11% of total revenue from just 0.69% of website sessions, achieving the highest Revenue Efficiency Index of 7.40. Its Average Order Value of $518.56 also leads all eight marketing channels. Investigate the campaigns, audiences, and placements responsible for this performance before expanding investment.

Measure: Conversion rate, average order value, revenue per session, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend once advertising cost data becomes available.

02

Strengthen High-Performing Referral Partnerships

Referral traffic contributes 42.20% of total revenue from just 11.59% of website sessions. With 5,514 transactions, a conversion rate of 5.28%, and a Revenue Efficiency Index of 3.64, Referral is a major contributor to marketing performance. Identify the referral sources responsible for these results, strengthen successful partnerships, and investigate opportunities to expand similar acquisition relationships.

Measure: Referral conversion rate, transaction volume, revenue contribution, revenue per session, and partner-level acquisition costs where available.

03

Improve Organic Search Conversion Efficiency

Organic Search generates 42.19% of total website sessions, making it the largest traffic acquisition channel. However, it contributes only 21.26% of total revenue, with a conversion rate of 0.94% and a Revenue Efficiency Index of 0.50. Investigate search intent, landing-page relevance, user experience, and conversion pathways. Conduct A/B tests to identify opportunities to improve conversion performance.

Measure: Organic conversion rate, revenue per session, landing-page engagement, transaction volume, and revenue contribution.

04

Improve Social Media Traffic Quality and Conversion

Social generates 25.07% of total website sessions, representing 225,904 visits, but contributes only 0.32% of total revenue. With 131 transactions, a conversion rate of 0.06%, and a Revenue Efficiency Index of approximately 0.01, the channel shows a significant gap between traffic acquisition and directly attributed revenue. Investigate audience targeting, campaign objectives, landing-page relevance, and conversion pathways. Evaluate assisted conversions and attribution before making decisions about future investment.

Measure: Social conversion rate, revenue per session, assisted conversions, transaction volume, and cost per acquisition where available.
